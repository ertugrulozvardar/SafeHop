### Posting tips
- Pick the first frame (the question) as the cover.
- Share each Reel to your Story too, with a **link sticker** to the App Store page.
- The same videos work on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
- Use an App Store **campaign link** (App Store Connect → App Analytics → Campaigns) per platform, e.g. `ct=instagram`, to see which channel brings downloads.
- Keep the tone factual ("reported incidents"), not "most dangerous neighborhoods".
