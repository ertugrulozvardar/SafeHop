SafeHop

Neighborhood safety for travelers.

This site hosts the data feed used by the SafeHop app: 6-month summaries of incidents reported to police in Chicago, Los Angeles, New York City, Miami and Seattle, built from official open-data portals and refreshed daily.

How it works

SafeHop counts incidents reported to police over the last 6 months and divides them by each area’s size. Areas are compared only with other areas in the same city: green is the third with the fewest reports per km², red the third with the most. SafeHop shows reported incidents, not a guarantee of safety. In an emergency, call 911.
